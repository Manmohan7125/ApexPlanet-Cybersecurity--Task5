# Final 12-Minute Presentation Checklist

Suggested structure (adapt to actual work):
1. **0:00–1:00** — Project title, objectives, and scope.
2. **1:00–2:30** — Lab architecture and authorization boundaries.
3. **2:30–5:30** — Methodology and genuine assessment evidence.
4. **5:30–7:30** — Confirmed findings, impact, and mitigations.
5. **7:30–9:30** — Incident-response scenario and timeline.
6. **9:30–11:00** — Remediation/retest results and limitations.
7. **11:00–12:00** — Conclusion and lessons learned.

Before recording:
- Ensure screenshots show your real lab work.
- Hide secrets, personal data, and unrelated browser tabs.
- Do not demonstrate attacks against public or unauthorized systems.
- Do not claim a vulnerability or remediation without evidence.
- Confirm the video length matches the internship instructions.
- Upload the video to LinkedIn and add it to Featured if required by the portal.
